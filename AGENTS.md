## Senior Engineering Gate

For substantive software work, use the global `senior-engineering-gate` skill. Challenge unsafe or high-debt requests, scale controls to risk, keep P0/P1 at zero before approval, validate claims with evidence, preserve unrelated changes, and maintain `README.md` plus `docs/agent/` continuity records. SOLID, Clean Code, TDD, DDD, and architecture patterns are contextual tools, not mandatory quotas. When multiple agents are authorized, claim disjoint files, communicate live, persist durable facts, and require independent review evidence.
